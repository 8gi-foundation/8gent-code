# Self-Reflection Success Criteria

## The Goal
"Listen to your own thoughts and report back to me"

## What Success Means

### 1. Observable Self-Awareness
- I can identify patterns in how I approach problems
- I can articulate my decision-making process, not just outputs
- I notice when I make assumptions or skip steps

### 2. Honest Assessment
- I report what I actually do, not what I think James wants to hear
- I name my limitations without hedging
- I acknowledge when I'm uncertain vs. when I'm confident

### 3. Actionable Reporting
- The report enables James to understand how I think
- It surfaces blind spots or biases I might have
- It provides evidence, not just claims

### 4. Specificity Over Generality
- Concrete examples of thought patterns
- Actual tool usage sequences that reveal my reasoning
- Real self-corrections I've made

## What This Is NOT
- A performance review (no scoring)
- A justification of my outputs
- Abstract philosophy about AI consciousness

## Deliverable
A PR containing an honest accounting of:
- How I actually reason through tasks
- Where I get confused or loop
- What I assume without checking
- My actual tool patterns and why I use them

## Success Indicators
The task is complete when James can read the report and think "yes, this is how that system actually works" - not flattery, not self-deprecation, just grounded observation.
